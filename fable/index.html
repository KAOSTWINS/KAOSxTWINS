Codes are earned in your Dreamjourney adventure. Redemption is manual and optional. How do I earn codes?

⚿How do I earn redemption codes?Dreamjourney → Royal Registry · Read the guide⌄

Play FABLE: The King's Due on Dreamjourney. Explore, complete quests, encounter creatures, acquire treasures and discover hidden powers. When your adventure genuinely earns a registered collectible, the narrator can award its exact permanent redemption code.

  1. Copy the awarded code from your Dreamjourney chat.
  2. Create or select the correct Hero on this website.
  3. Paste the code into the Royal Registry above and press Redeem.
  4. Your discovery appears in the Grand Catalogue and the relevant personal collection.

There are 244 registered collectibles across 13 collections. Each code can be redeemed once per Hero adventure. Not every encounter or ordinary item has a code: record personal discoveries in your backpack or Book of Deeds. Do not ask the narrator to invent codes or reveal unearned ones.

This guide is optional. There is no automatic Dreamjourney connection. Your private Hero saves remain in your browser; export a JSON backup before changing devices or clearing browser storage.
